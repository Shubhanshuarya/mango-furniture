Products & care

What is mango wood?

A dense, durable hardwood with a rich, varied grain. Because every board is different, no two pieces are exactly alike.

How do I care for my furniture?

Clean regularly with a soft dry or slightly damp cloth and avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. Wipe spills immediately. Keep pieces away from prolonged direct sunlight and heat sources, and periodically check fittings.

How do I care for leather seats?

Top-grain leather wipes clean easily with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh cleaners and direct heat.
