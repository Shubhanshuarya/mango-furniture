Shipping policy

ShippingRefundPrivacyTerms of service

Order processing

All orders are processed within 1–4 business days (excluding weekends and holidays). Orders placed after the cut-off time, 2 PM PST, will be processed the next business day.

You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been shipped.

Shipping rates & delivery estimates

We may call you to provide a quote if shipping is higher than our estimated rates.

Estimated delivery times: Domestic shipping: 3–10 business days. Delivery delays may occasionally occur due to unforeseen circumstances.

Free shipping

We offer free shipping on products; however, we may call you to provide a quote if shipping is more than anticipated.

Shipment confirmation & tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. Tracking details may take up to 24 hours to become active.

Delivery issues

If your package is delayed, lost, or damaged, please contact us at info@mangofurnitureinc.com within 2 days of delivery. We will assist you in resolving the issue as quickly as possible.

Incorrect shipping information

Please ensure that your shipping address is accurate. We are not responsible for orders delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

Undelivered packages

If a package is returned to us due to failed delivery attempts, additional shipping charges may apply for re-shipment.

Contact us

For any shipping-related queries, please reach out to us at info@mangofurnitureinc.com or call 530-443-7964.
